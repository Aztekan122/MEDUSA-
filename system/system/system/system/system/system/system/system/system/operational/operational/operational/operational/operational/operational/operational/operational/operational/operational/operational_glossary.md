# MEDUSA Operational Glossary

This glossary defines key operational terms used throughout MEDUSA’s mission documentation.  
It ensures DIU reviewers have a consistent understanding of MEDUSA’s operational language, workflows, and mission concepts.

---

## 1. Mission Analyst
Operator responsible for interpreting fused outputs, analytics, behaviour models, and confidence scoring.

---

## 2. System Integrator
Operator responsible for ICD alignment, external system configuration, and deployment model validation.

---

## 3. Field Operator
Operator responsible for running edge or embedded deployments during active missions.

---

## 4. Configuration Profile
A predefined operational setup specifying sensors, deployment models, pipeline depth, and mission parameters.

---

## 5. Mission Replay
A post-mission reconstruction of fused sensor data, analytics outputs, and behaviour models for analysis.

---

## 6. Degraded Mode
A fallback operational state activated when sensors, networks, or compute resources degrade.

---

## 7. GNSS-Denied Mode
Operational mode used when GPS/GNSS signals are unavailable or unreliable.

---

## 8. Manual Override
An operator-triggered action that adjusts pipeline behaviour, fusion weighting, or operational mode.

---

## 9. Operator Alert
A notification indicating sensor dropout, network degradation, GNSS-denial, or pipeline fallback activation.

---

## 10. Behaviour Model
An analytic output describing patterns, trends, or inferred behaviours from fused sensor data.

---

## 11. Operational Continuity
The ability of MEDUSA to maintain mission functionality despite degraded conditions.

---

## 12. Fallback Pathway
A predefined operational route activated when standard processing cannot continue.

---

## 13. Mission Environment
The physical, electromagnetic, and network conditions under which MEDUSA operates.

---

## 14. Deployment Model
The operational environment: cloud, edge, embedded, or hybrid.

---

## 15. Operational Throughput
The rate at which mission data is processed during active operations.

---

## 16. Operator Workflow
The structured sequence of actions operators follow during pre-mission, active mission, and post-mission phases.

---

## 17. Alert Severity Level
Classification of operator alerts based on mission impact: informational, warning, critical.

---

## 18. Operational Latency
The time required for MEDUSA to produce mission outputs under specific deployment conditions.

---

## Purpose
To provide DIU reviewers with a consistent terminology reference for MEDUSA’s operational documentation, ensuring clarity and alignment across mission workflows.
