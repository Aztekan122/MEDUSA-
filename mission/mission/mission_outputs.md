# MEDUSA Mission Outputs

This document defines the mission-level outputs generated by MEDUSA during pre‑mission, active mission, and post‑mission phases.  
It ensures DIU reviewers understand the actionable intelligence MEDUSA provides to operators and external systems.

---

## 1. Fused Sensor Outputs
MEDUSA produces unified multi-sensor representations combining:

- Visual streams  
- Acoustic signatures  
- RF emissions  
- Thermal imagery  
- Positional data  
- Metadata fields  

Outcome:  
Operators receive a coherent, mission-ready fused view of the environment.

---

## 2. Threat Classifications
MEDUSA identifies mission-relevant threats using:

- Multi-sensor fusion  
- Behaviour modelling  
- Confidence scoring  
- Partial-data inference  

Threat outputs include:

- Object classification  
- Activity recognition  
- Anomaly detection  
- Pattern deviation alerts  

Outcome:  
Operators gain real-time situational awareness.

---

## 3. Behaviour Models
MEDUSA generates behaviour models describing:

- Movement patterns  
- Temporal trends  
- Environmental interactions  
- Predictive behaviour signatures  

Outcome:  
Mission analysts can interpret deeper patterns beyond raw sensor data.

---

## 4. Confidence Scores
Each fused output and analytic result includes:

- Reliability weighting  
- Sensor health contribution  
- Degradation impact  
- Partial-data adjustments  

Outcome:  
Operators understand the trust level of each mission output.

---

## 5. Operational Alerts
MEDUSA provides alerts for:

- Sensor dropout  
- GNSS-denial  
- Network degradation  
- Pipeline fallback activation  
- Legacy adapter failures  

Outcome:  
Operators receive clear, actionable mission notifications.

---

## 6. Mission Logs
MEDUSA records:

- Ingestion events  
- Fusion decisions  
- Analytics outputs  
- Degraded-mode transitions  
- Operator overrides  

Outcome:  
Complete mission traceability.

---

## 7. Replay Datasets
Post-mission replay includes:

- Timestamped sensor streams  
- Fused outputs  
- Analytics results  
- Behaviour models  
- Operator interactions  

Outcome:  
Analysts can reconstruct missions with full fidelity.

---

## 8. Exportable Mission Intelligence
MEDUSA supports export of:

- Fused representations  
- Threat classifications  
- Behaviour models  
- Confidence metrics  
- Operational logs  
- Replay datasets  

Outcome:  
Mission intelligence can be shared with external systems or archived.

---

## Purpose
To provide DIU reviewers with a clear understanding of MEDUSA’s mission outputs, demonstrating actionable intelligence, traceability, and mission readiness.
