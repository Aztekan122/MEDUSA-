# MEDUSA Mission Glossary

This glossary defines mission-level terminology used throughout MEDUSA’s mission documentation.  
It ensures DIU reviewers have a consistent understanding of mission concepts, actors, phases, and outputs.

---

## 1. Mission Use Case
A structured description of how MEDUSA supports a specific defence mission scenario.

---

## 2. Mission Scenario
A realistic, detailed example of MEDUSA operating in a real-world mission environment.

---

## 3. Mission Flow
A chronological sequence describing how MEDUSA behaves across pre‑mission, active mission, and post‑mission phases.

---

## 4. Mission Requirements
Operational, environmental, and behavioural conditions MEDUSA must satisfy during mission execution.

---

## 5. Mission Outputs
Fused sensor data, analytics, behaviour models, confidence scores, alerts, logs, and replay datasets produced during missions.

---

## 6. Mission Actors
Entities interacting with MEDUSA, including operators, external systems, mission assets, and environmental inputs.

---

## 7. Mission Replay
A post‑mission reconstruction of sensor streams, fused outputs, analytics, and operator interactions.

---

## 8. Mission Intelligence
Actionable outputs such as threat classifications, behaviour models, fused representations, and confidence scores.

---

## 9. Mission Environment
Physical, electromagnetic, and network conditions affecting mission execution.

---

## 10. Mission Continuity
MEDUSA’s ability to maintain operational behaviour despite degraded conditions.

---

## 11. Mission Degradation
Any reduction in sensor availability, network stability, compute resources, or environmental clarity.

---

## 12. Mission Fallback
A predefined pathway MEDUSA activates when standard processing cannot continue.

---

## 13. Mission Autonomy
MEDUSA’s ability to operate without external connectivity, especially in edge or embedded deployments.

---

## 14. Mission Logs
Timestamped records of ingestion events, fusion decisions, analytics outputs, degraded-mode transitions, and operator actions.

---

## 15. Mission Confidence Score
A reliability metric applied to fused outputs and analytics results.

---

## Purpose
To provide DIU reviewers with a consistent terminology reference for MEDUSA’s mission documentation, ensuring clarity and alignment across mission workflows.
