# MEDUSA Mission Review Checklist

This checklist provides DIU reviewers with a concise way to verify the completeness, consistency, and readiness of MEDUSA’s mission documentation.

---

## 1. Mission Use Cases
- [ ] Mission use cases are clearly defined  
- [ ] Scenarios reflect realistic defence environments  
- [ ] Mission flows cover pre-, active-, and post-mission phases  

---

## 2. Mission Requirements
- [ ] Environmental requirements are documented  
- [ ] Sensor requirements follow ICD structures  
- [ ] Network and compute requirements are defined  
- [ ] Operator requirements are clear and actionable  

---

## 3. Mission Constraints & Assumptions
- [ ] Environmental constraints are realistic  
- [ ] Sensor constraints reflect operational variability  
- [ ] Network and compute constraints are defined  
- [ ] Operator and external system assumptions are documented  

---

## 4. Mission Outputs
- [ ] Fused sensor outputs are described  
- [ ] Threat classifications and analytics outputs are defined  
- [ ] Behaviour models and confidence scores are included  
- [ ] Mission logs and replay datasets are documented  
- [ ] Exportable intelligence formats are defined  

---

## 5. Mission Validation & Testing
- [ ] Ingestion validation is defined  
- [ ] Fusion validation is complete  
- [ ] Analytics validation covers classification, behaviour, and anomalies  
- [ ] Degraded-mode validation is included  
- [ ] Operator interaction validation is documented  
- [ ] Replay and export validation are complete  
- [ ] Full mission test plan is present  

---

## 6. Mission Risks & Edge Cases
- [ ] Mission risk register is complete  
- [ ] Mitigation strategies are documented  
- [ ] Edge-case behaviour is defined  
- [ ] GNSS-denial, sensor failure, network partitioning, and compute collapse are covered  

---

## 7. Mission Metrics & Limits
- [ ] Mission success metrics are defined  
- [ ] Operational limits are documented  
- [ ] Metrics cover ingestion, fusion, analytics, degraded-mode, and operator workflows  

---

## 8. Mission Summary & Closing
- [ ] Mission summary provides a clear overview  
- [ ] Closing statement reflects mission readiness  
- [ ] Documentation is consistent across all mission files  

---

## Purpose
To provide DIU reviewers with a structured checklist ensuring MEDUSA’s mission documentation is complete, consistent, and ready for evaluation.
