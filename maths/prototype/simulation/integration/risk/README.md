# MEDUSA Risk Register

This directory contains MEDUSA’s risk analysis, including technical, operational, integration, and development risks.  
It provides structured mitigation pathways and dependency tracking to demonstrate responsible engineering practice.

## Contents
- Technical risk register
- Integration and interoperability risks
- Algorithmic and performance risks
- Development and timeline risks
- External dependency analysis
- Mitigation strategies and contingency plans

## Purpose
To give DIU reviewers a clear understanding of MEDUSA’s risk landscape and the proactive steps taken to ensure reliable development, integration, and deployment.
